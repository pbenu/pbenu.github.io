This layout supports footnotes, which is the main thing academic writing needs
and most blog templates get wrong.[^1] Write them in markdown and they collect
at the foot of the page with return links.

You can cite in whatever style your field uses.[^morris]

[^1]: A footnote looks like this in the source. The marker can be a number or a word — the word is easier to keep track of in a long piece.
[^morris]: C. J. Morris, writing in 1933, is an example of the kind of source that wants a proper note rather than an inline aside.
